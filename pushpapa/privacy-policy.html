Push Papa Privacy Policy

Applies to version 1.4 and later.

Normal gameplay works offline. The developer does not collect personal information or gameplay history from this app. Saved progress and settings are stored on your device. The app does not use advertising, usage analytics, or automatic crash reporting.

Game Center (iOS)

The iOS app supports Apple's Game Center. Game Center handles sign-in and receives scores and achievement progress for leaderboards and achievements. The developer does not send or store this information on a separate server. See Game Center & Privacy for Apple's data practices.

Sharing

The share button lets you send game results and other prepared text to a sharing destination you choose. The developer does not collect the shared content or your account information at the destination. The destination service's privacy policy applies to its handling of that content.
